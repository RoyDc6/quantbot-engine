# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in QuantBot Engine a respectful, harassment-free experience regardless of background, identity, experience level, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

Community members should:

- communicate with empathy, patience, and technical clarity;
- critique ideas and evidence rather than people;
- disclose assumptions, uncertainty, and conflicts of interest;
- respect privacy, credentials, proprietary data, and responsible-disclosure boundaries;
- avoid presenting research output as guaranteed performance or personalized financial advice;
- accept maintainer decisions about project scope and execution safety.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate disclosure of private information, market manipulation, credential sharing, spam, and attempts to pressure contributors into unsafe trading behavior are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other participation that violates this policy, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately when appropriate, and protect reporters from retaliation.

For a sensitive conduct report, contact the primary maintainer through the contact method on the [`RoyDc6` GitHub profile](https://github.com/RoyDc6). Do not include private evidence in a public issue.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository spaces and in public interactions where someone is representing the project.
